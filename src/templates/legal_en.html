2. Principle: data minimisation

This website works without cookies, without analytics or tracking services and without embedded third-party content. Fonts, scripts and images are loaded exclusively from my own server. Simply opening the pages transmits no data to anyone other than my hosting provider (section 3).

3. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by STRATO AG, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany. When you open a page, STRATO automatically processes the information your browser transmits on my behalf and stores it in server log files: IP address, date and time of access, file requested, amount of data transferred, referrer URL, browser type and operating system.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is the secure and stable operation of the website and the prevention of attacks. The log files are deleted automatically by the host after a short period and are not combined with other data. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with STRATO. Further information: strato.de/datenschutz.

4. Contact form and email

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are transmitted to a script on my web server, which immediately forwards them to me by email. Nothing is stored on the server. My mailbox is provided by Yahoo (Yahoo EMEA Limited, Dublin, Ireland). The same applies if you email me directly.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request is aimed at a contract, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries). I keep your details for processing and possible follow-up questions for up to six months after the correspondence ends and then delete them, unless statutory retention obligations apply or a contract is concluded.

To protect against spam, the form checks an additional field that is invisible to people and the time taken to submit. No data is transmitted to third parties and no profiles are created.

5. Local storage (dark mode)

If you switch dark mode on or off, that setting is stored in your browser’s local storage (key “theme”). This is necessary to provide the display you explicitly requested and is permitted without consent under Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG. The setting never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time via your browser settings.
