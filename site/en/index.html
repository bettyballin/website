Services

Four areas that rarely show up separately in practice – so I offer them together.

External data protection officer

Appointment as external DPO under Art. 37 GDPR, with fixed availability for your team and a direct line for customers and the supervisory authority.

  • Records of processing activities and deletion concept
  • Processor agreements, sub-processors, third-country transfers (SCCs, TIAs)
  • Data protection impact assessments
  • Data subject requests and data breaches
  • Training for employees

Information security and compliance

An ISMS that grows with the company instead of sitting in a drawer – policies, risk assessment, supplier management, incident response and regular exercises.

  • ISO 27001-oriented policies and risk management
  • NIS2 applicability checks and measures
  • Business continuity and tabletop exercises
  • Customer security questionnaires and audits, incl. BSI C5 requirements

AI governance

Assessing AI tools and agents from a data protection perspective, writing usage policies people actually follow, and classifying systems under the EU AI Act – with an understanding of how the models really work.

  • Data protection assessment of AI tools and vendor contracts
  • AI usage policies for teams
  • EU AI Act classification and obligations
  • Data flows, prompt hygiene and retention

Software security

Security code reviews, threat modelling and vulnerability assessments, drawn from several years of AI security research. Privacy by design becomes an architecture decision instead of a retrofit.

  • Code reviews and triage of security findings
  • Threat modelling for new features
  • Privacy by design and by default
  • Technical and organisational measures

Approach

This is how an engagement typically runs:

  1. Assessment

    Review processing activities, systems, contracts and existing documentation – remotely or on site. The result is an honest list of gaps, not a list of fears.

  2. Action plan

    Prioritised by risk and effort, with clear owners and realistic dates. Anything that touches the product is planned with the development team, not around it.

  3. Ongoing support

    Fixed availability, reviews of new features, tools and contracts, training, and documentation that stands up to customer audits.
