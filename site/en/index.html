Approach

This is how a project typically runs:

  1. Conversation and requirements

    What should be built, what exists already, what are the budget and time constraints? The result is a short, readable quote – not a 40-page specification.

  2. Delivery in short steps

    Show something that runs early, work in feedback, continue. Security and data protection are part of the work from the first line, not an afterthought.

  3. Handover and support

    Documentation, onboarding, all credentials in your hands. Afterwards, if you like: maintenance, further development or the role of external data protection officer.
